📐 Formulas Used

Feed Rate (IPM) = RPM × Number of Flutes × Chip Load (rounded up to nearest 5)
Plunge Rate (IPM) = Feed Rate × 0.5 (50% of feed rate, rounded up to nearest 5)
Depth per Pass = Bit Diameter × Material Factor
Stepover % = Based on operation type and bit geometry
Ramp Angle = Based on material hardness (1.5°–5°) and bit geometry
Ramp Distance = Depth per Pass ÷ tan(Ramp Angle)
